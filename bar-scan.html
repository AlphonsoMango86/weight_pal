Plate colours

Gym colours vary. Use ⌖ then tap a plate in the picture to teach the app that plate’s colour. Untick any colour that confuses the scan. Plates of the same colour (e.g. two black sizes) are told apart by height, so fill in the Ø column (plate diameter in mm, or any consistent size units) for them. Keep the scan line on the bar’s centre line.
